2021-09-13 ​Administrative penalty order issued in terms of section 167 (1) of the Financial Sector Regulation Act, 2017 regarding contravention of Section 30(1)(a) and 31(1) of the Insurance Act No. 18 of 2017 (Insurance Act). The penalty arises from the following contraventions of the Insurance Act:
a. AVBOB failed to adopt, implement and document an effective governance framework in terms of section 30(1)(a) of the Insurance Act due to the failure of the board of directors in their oversight role over the board nominations process as prescribed in the AVBOB Mutual Assurance Society Incorporation (Private) Act, 7 of 1951 (respectively "the AVBOB Act" and "the Regulations"). This is so as the board of directors failed to detect the irregularities in the nomination of directors which protracted for a period of three years in 2017, 2018 and 2019.
b. Avbob failed to have procedures in place to identify non-compliance with section 30 of the Insurance Act as required in terms of section 31(1) of the Insurance Act. This is so as no effective governance framework was adopted and implemented by the insurer which led to the irregularities in the board nomination process over a three-year period in 2017, 2018 and 2019, not being picked up. 		 AVBOB Mutual Assurance Society 

Penalty of R5 of which R3 million rand of the administrative penalty will be suspended for a

period of three years and the remaining balance of R2 million rand is payable within 14 working days from the date of the penalty order.

2024-09-11

Escap SOC Limited contravened section 5(4) of the Insurance Act No. 18 of 2017, the Prudential Standard GOI 2: Governance of Insurers, as well as the Prudential Standard GOI 5 as it:

  • conducted business other than insurance business without the approval of the PA.
  • Failed to notify the PA of the outsourcing of a material function.
  • Failed to have a written contract for outsourcing a material business activity.
  • failure by management to review their outsourcing arrangement.
  • Failed to adopt, implement and document an effective governance framework in terms of section 30(1)(a) of the Insurance Act by outsourcing a material business activity without the contractual requirements (agreement and service level agreement) per paragraph 8 of the Prudential Standard GOI 5 being in place, and with no proper oversight from the Board.
  • failed to have procedures in place to identify any non-compliance with section 30(1) of the Insurance Act.
  • failure of the board to provide oversight over senior management to ensure that senior management was managing the affairs of the insurer in accordance with the strategies and policies set by the board of directors.
