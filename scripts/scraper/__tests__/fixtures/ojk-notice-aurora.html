​Indonesia Financial Services Authority (OJK) herewith announces the results of the Formal Investigation in the case of violations of Capital Market regulations by PT Aurora Asset Management.

Considering the role and involvementof the relevant Parties in the violations and in order to create a deterrence effect for financial services industry participants, on October 30th, 2026, OJK imposes administrative sanctions and/or written instruction as follows:

  1. OJK imposes PT Aurora Asset Management Administrative Sanctions in form of Penalty of Rp2.575.000.000,00 (two billion five hundred seventy-five million rupiah) and a written instruction to settle the dissolution/liquidation of Mutual Funds as follows:

    a. Aurora SMC Equity Mutual Fund;

    b. Aurora Syariah Saham Amanah Sharia Mutual Fund;

    c. Aurora Dana Ekuitas Mutual Fund;

    d. Aurora Sharia Equity Sharia Mutual Fund; and

    e. Aurora Equity Mutual Fund.

    within 6 (six) months from the date of issuance of the administrative sanction letter. In fulfilling the written instruction, PT Aurora Asset Management is required to continue reporting its progress to OJK.

    OJK also orders the relevant custodian bank to carry out the dissolution of the relevant Mutual Funds. In addition, OJK may also impose other administrative measures in connection with the dissolution of the relevant Mutual Funds.

  2. The Administrative Sanctions in the form of a Penalty and the written instruction were imposed because PT Aurora Asset Management was proven to have committed the following violations:

    a. The provisions of Article 24 of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016 juncto Article 2 paragraphs (1) and (2) of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016, because PT Aurora Asset Management failed to pay redemption requests submitted by investors of the Aurora Equity Mutual Fund in accordance with the applicable provisions.

    b. The provisions of Article 6 paragraph (1) letter d of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016 juncto Article 7 paragraph (2) and Article 8 paragraph (2) of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016, because PT Aurora Asset Management did not adjust the composition of its securities portfolio, which comprised Securities issued by 1(one) party exceeding 10% (ten percent) of the Net Asset Value of the Mutual Funds managed by PT Management Aurora Asset until the imposition of sanctions by OJK. 

    c. The provisions of Article 14 paragraph (1) and Article 15 of POJK 33/POJK.04/2019 Number juncto Article 8 paragraph (2) of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016, because PT Aurora Asset Management did not adjust the composition of Securities issued by 1 (one) party exceeding 20% (twenty percent) of the Net Asset Value of the Aurora Sharia Equity Sharia Mutual Fund and the Aurora Syariah SahamAmanahSharia Mutual Fund, and continued to commit the violation until the imposition of sanctions by OJK.

    d. The provisions of Article 45 letter d juncto Article 48 of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016, because PT Aurora Asset Management did not dissolve the following Mutual Funds in accordance with the applicable provisions: Aurora Dana Ekuitas Mutual Fund, Aurora Syariah Saham Amanah Sharia Mutual Fund, Aurora ETF FTSE Indonesia ESG Index Mutual Fund, Aurora Likuid Mutual Fund, and Aurora Likuid Syariah Sharia Mutual Fund, and continued to commit the violation until the imposition of sanctions by OJK.

    e. The provisions of Article 37 paragraph (2) letter a and Article 44 paragraphs (1) and (2) of POJK 43/POJK.04/2015, Number which have been revoked and re regulated under Article 58 paragraph (2) and Article 68 paragraphs (1) and (2) of POJK Number 17/POJK.04/2022, because PT Aurora Asset Management engaged in the sale or marketing of Mutual Funds by promising investors or prospective investors a specific rate of return within a specified period.

    f. The provisions of Article 27 paragraph (1) of the Law of the Republic of Indonesia Number 8 of 1995 concerning the Capital Market junctis Article 18, Article 19, Article 25, Article 28, Article 33, and Article 34 paragraph (1) of POJK 43/POJK.04/2015 regulated in Number as Article re 23 paragraph (1), Article 31 letter a, Article 36, Article 42, and Article 43 paragraph (1) letter a of POJK Number 17/POJK.04/2022, because PT Aurora Asset Management failed to perform its duties to the best of its ability in the interests of the Mutual Funds, including by failing to execute securities transactions on behalf of the Mutual Funds under the best available conditions, as evidenced by cross transactions between Mutual Funds at prices outside the applicable stock exchange price range. In addition, transactions were conducted with affiliated parties at prices that did not reflect the best conditions available at the time of the transactions.

    g. The provisions of Article 27 paragraph (1) of Law of the Republic of Indonesia Number 8 of 1995 concerning the Capital Market juncto Articles 2, 7, and 18 of POJK Number 43/POJK.04/2015, as re regulated under Article 2 letters a, b, and c, Article 8 letters a and b, and Article 23 paragraph (1) of POJK Number 17/POJK.04/2022, as well as Article 2 paragraph (1) and Article 4 paragraph (1) of POJK Number 10/POJK.04/2018, because PT Aurora Asset Management conducted investment management and Mutual Fund marketing activities based on instructions from another party and not in accordance with the applicable provisions.

    h. The provisions of Article 2 paragraph (1), Article 34 paragraphs (1) and (2), and Article 35 paragraph (6) letter a of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016, Article 2 of POJK Number 39/POJK.04/2014 concerning Mutual Fund Selling Agents juncto Article 31 of Law of the Republic of Indonesia Number 8 of 1995 concerning the Capital Market, and Article 2 paragraph (1) of POJK Number 45/POJK.04/2016, because PT Aurora Asset Management marketed Mutual Funds using marketing personnel (referral personnel) who were not licensed as Mutual Fund Selling Agents (APERD) or Mutual Fund Selling Agent Representatives (WAPERD), with such referral personnel appointed by the Shareholder. Furthermore, the marketing of Mutual Funds by the referral personnel was not supported by a written agreement.

  3. OJK imposes Mr. Piter Rasiman, as the Shareholder of PT Aurora Asset Management a prohibition from becoming a member of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Shareholders and/or managementof companies in the Financial Services Sector for 20 (twenty) years, effective from the date of issuance of the prohibition letter, because he was proven to have caused PT Aurora Asset Management to commit the violations referred to in point 2 (two) above.

  4. OJK imposes on Mrs. Ferra, as the President Director of PT Aurora Asset Management, the following sanctions and measures:

    a. administrative sanction in the form of a Penalty of Rp610,000,000.00 (six hundred ten million rupiah);

    b. written instruction to carry out the dissolution of the Mutual Funds managed by PT Aurora Asset Management in accordance with OJK’s written instruction to PT Aurora Asset Management; and

    c. prohibition on becoming a member of the Board of Directors or the Board of Commissioners, and a shareholder of PT Aurora Asset Managemen, the prohibition shall take effect upon completion of the dissolution of the Mutual Funds,

    These sanctions and measures are imposed because Mrs. Ferra was proven to have caused PT Aurora Asset Management to commit the violations referred to in point 2 (two) above.

  5. OJK imposes on Mr. Manuel ManahanMaleaki, as aDirector of PT Aurora Asset Management, the following sanctions and measures:

    a. administrative sanction in the form of a Penalty of Rp610,000,000.00 (six hundred ten million rupiah);

    b. written instruction to carry out the dissolution of the Mutual Funds managed by PT Aurora Asset Management in accordance with OJK’s written instruction to PT Aurora Asset Management; and

    c. prohibition on becoming a member of the Board of Directors or the Board of Commissioners, and a shareholder of PT Aurora Asset Management, the prohibition shall take effect upon completion of the dissolution of the Mutual Funds,

    These sanctions and measures are imposed because Mr. Manuel Manahan Maleaki was proven to have caused PT Aurora Asset Management to commit the violations referred to in point 2 (two) above.
