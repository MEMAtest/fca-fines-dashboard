​Indonesia Financial Services Authority (OJK​) herewith announces the results of the Formal Investigation in the case of violations of Capital Market regulations by PT Berlian Aset Manajemen.

Considering the role and involvement of each Persons in the violations and in order to create deterrence effect for financial services industry players, on October 13th, 2023 OJK imposes administrative sanctions and/or Written Order as follows:

1. OJK imposes PT Berlian Aset Manajemen (PT BAM) penalty of Rp525.000.000,00 (five hundred and twenty five million million rupiahs) and Written Order to immediately complete the dissolution process of Reksa Dana Berlian Khatulistiwa Saham and pay the liquidation proceeds which are the rights of the Participation Unit Holders within a period of no later than 6 (six) months.

Furthermore, PT BAM must report all progress related to the implementation of the Written Orders every month to OJK. If within that 6 (six) months period PT BAM does not carry out the Written Orders, it will be subject to Administrative Sanctions in the form of the Revocation of the Investment Manager License of PT BAM.

The administrative sanctions in form of penalty and written order were impose by OJK to PT BAM because PT BAM was proven to have committed the following violations:

a. The Provisions of Article 24 of POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016 because PT BAM made payments for buying back participation units (redemption debt) that did not comply with the provisions, namely more than 7 (seven) trading days since the buyback order was received by PT BAM.

b. The provisions of Article 6 paragraph (1) letter d POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016 as amended and regulated are the same as in POJK Number 2/POJK.04/2020 jis. Article 7 paragraph (2) and Article 8 paragraph (2) POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016, because PT BAM in managing Reksa Dana Berlian Khatulistiwa Campuran and Reksa Dana Berlian Khatulistiwa Saham has a portfolio of securities issued by 1 (one) party that is more than 10% (ten percent) of NAV and PT BAM does not adjust the composition of the Securities portfolio within the time limit according to the provisions.

c. The provisions of Article 6 paragraph (1) letter j POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016 as amended and regulated are the same in POJK Number 2/POJK.04/2020 jo and Article 8 paragraph (2) POJK Number 23/POJK.04 /2016 because PT BAM in managing Reksa Dana Berlian Khatulistiwa Saham has a portfolio of securities issued by parties affiliated with PT BAM, namely HOTL and ALTO Securities of more than 20% (twenty percent) of NAV and PT BAM does not adjust the composition of the Securities portfolio within the time limit according to the provisions.

2. OJK imposes Mrs. Retno Dewi as President Director of PT BAM and Mr. Arsoni Chrinarto Malau as Director of PT BAM penalty of Rp125.000.000,00 (one hundred twenty five million rupiah) join responsibility and written instructions to complete the Written Order imposed on PT BAM to immediately complete the process of dissolving the Reksa Dana Berlian Khatulistiwa Saham and pay the liquidation proceeds which are the rights of the Participation Unit Holders and submit the dissolution report to OJK for being proven to be the party that caused PT BAM to commit violation as referred to in number 1 letters a, b, and c above and being proven to have violated the provisions of number 3 letter b number 1) letter g) and letter h) Regulation Number V.A.3.

3. OJK imposes PT Bank Central Asia Tbk. As the Custodian penalty of Rp100.000.000,00 (one hundred million rupiah) for being proven to have violated Article 8 paragraph (1) and Article 8 paragraph (3) POJK Number 23/POJK.04/2016​.​
