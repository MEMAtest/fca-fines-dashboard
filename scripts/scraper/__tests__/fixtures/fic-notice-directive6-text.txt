                                        NOTICE OF SANCTION

 ACCEPTANCE OF ADMINISTRATIVE SANCTION ISSUED IN TERMS OF SECTION
   45C(3)(e) OF THE FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE CENTRE ACT, ACT 38 OF 2001

                                        JJ Smit Attorneys


1.      On 31 March 2023, the Centre issued Directive 6 of 2023, in terms whereof account-
        able institutions were directed to complete and submit its Risk and Compliance Re-
        turns by 31 May 2023.
2.      JJ Smit Attorneys (the accountable institution) has not complied with Directive 6 of
        2023.
3.      In the circumstances, and taking into consideration the nature of the non-compliance,
        the Centre is of the opinion that the accountable institution be directed to remediate
        their non-compliance by submitting the Risk and Compliance Return on or before 18
        September 2025 and further taking into consideration the nature of the non-compli-
        ance, the Centre is of the opinion that the accountable institution may pay an ac-
        ceptance of non-compliance penalty (admission of administrative non-compliance
        fine) to bring the matter to finality.
4.      Having considered the available facts and information, the Centre, hereby imposes a
        fine of R50 000 in terms of section 45C(3)(e) on the accountable institution for their
        failure to comply with Directive 6 of 2023 on or before 31 May 2023.
5.      However, if the institution submits the Risk and Compliance Return to the Centre on
        or before 9 September 2025, the institution may pay a reduced financial penalty of
        R10 000 which reduced penalty must be paid on or before 18 September 2025. (There-
        after the full R50 000 becomes due and payable)
6.      The fine is payable to:
        Account Name: NRF- FIC Sanctions
        Account Holder: National Treasury
        Account Number: 80552749
        Bank: South African Reserve Bank
